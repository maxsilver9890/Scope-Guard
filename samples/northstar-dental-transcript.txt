Northstar Dental discovery call notes

The owner expects a second dental clinic to open in approximately six weeks. We want the website ready before the second clinic opens, but we have some scheduling flexibility.

The website needs a homepage, separate pages for both clinic locations, service pages, an about page, and contact information. Each location page needs its address, hours, phone number, and a map.

Online appointment booking is preferred, but our receptionist is concerned about changing availability. A request form is another possibility. We have not decided which booking method to use.

We use DentDesk. Any integration depends on vendor and API confirmation, and the website must not replace DentDesk.

Online payments are not a launch requirement. Patients will pay at the clinic for now; we may consider online deposits later.

The website must work on mobile. Readable text and keyboard navigation are important, but we have not agreed to a formal accessibility standard.

The client will provide contact details and the logo.
Photos of the new clinic are not available yet.

Our marketing contact expects to write the service descriptions, but estimates about two weeks and is not certain.
Copywriting is not included unless separately approved.

We want basic SEO, including page titles, meta descriptions, and local-search considerations. Higher rankings would be great, but are not guaranteed. Ongoing SEO has not been agreed.

Our budget range is INR 80,000 to INR 100,000. The owner said it sounds reasonable, but written approval is pending.

The design should feel premium but approachable and use our dark-green and cream branding.

Patient accounts and access to or storage of medical records are explicitly excluded.
No medical records should be stored on the website.

Automated reminders might be useful later.
They are not part of the launch scope. Our receptionist will handle confirmations manually at launch.

We want fewer missed appointments, but we have not agreed to a quantified reduction or guaranteed outcome. The agency is preparing a proposed scope and must distinguish confirmed decisions from assumptions and open questions.
